    Get device details

    Reads the MAC address, open control ports, model, serial number and firmware where the device reports them, and offers ready-made control commands for recognised devices (PJLink, Epson, Panasonic, BenQ and Barco projectors, Samsung and LG displays, QSC Q-SYS, Lightware, Hikvision, Extron, Crestron, AMX). You are asked for a login only if the device needs one.

    How updating works

    When a new version is found you see what changed and are asked whether to update. Your devices, command mappings and settings are stored separately from the program, so they are kept. You can also update by running a newer Setup.exe over the existing install.
